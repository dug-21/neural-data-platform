## Executive Summary

The commercial building management market represents a massive opportunity for edge intelligence. With $190 billion wasted annually on inefficient energy use in buildings without automation, and traditional BMS costing $50,000-$500,000, there is a significant gap for affordable, intelligent solutions targeting the 90% of buildings without smart building functionality.

**Key Finding:** An edge intelligence platform at $75 could deliver 15-30% energy savings with ROI in 6-12 months - dramatically better than traditional 3-7 year payback periods for conventional BMS.

## Current Pain Points

### 1. Cost Barriers for Small-Medium Buildings

> "Building Management Systems have not been designed for small buildings. It's all about the scale! Big sized buildings of over 50,000 square feet are more likely to benefit."

**Traditional BMS Costs:**
- Entry level: $5,000-$50,000 (cloud-based)
- Mid-range: $50,000-$150,000 (30,000 sq ft building)
- Enterprise: $150,000-$500,000+
- Target future cost: $0.75/sq ft (5x less than current)

Source: [Frazier Service BEMS Costs](https://frazierservicecompany.com/assess-energy-management-system-costs/)

### 2. Interoperability and Vendor Lock-in

> "One of the most pressing issues in the BAS landscape is the lack of standardization across platforms and vendors. Many solutions rely on proprietary communication protocols."

**Problems:**
- Proprietary protocols prevent integration
- Inconsistent BACnet, KNX, Modbus implementations
- Vendor lock-in increases long-term costs
- Limited flexibility for upgrades

Source: [Mordor Intelligence BAS Market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/building-automation-system-market)

### 3. System Drift and Maintenance

> "A large percentage of buildings with installed BAS do not achieve their expected savings due to both improper configuration initially and 'system drift' over time."

**Pattern:**
- Year 1-2: Savings achieved as designed
- Year 3+: Savings drop off without maintenance
- Cause: Faulty equipment, outdated software, maintenance gaps

Source: [Propmodo BAS Analysis](https://propmodo.com/the-inconvenient-truth-about-building-automation-systems/)

### 4. Legacy System Integration

> "Many older buildings still rely on outdated HVAC, lighting, and security systems, which may not be easily compatible with modern BAS technologies."

**Challenges:**
- Costly retrofit requirements
- Time-consuming integration
- Skilled workforce shortage
- Multi-vendor complexity

### 5. Skilled Workforce Shortage

> "This fragmentation also raises challenges in hiring skilled professionals capable of managing diverse system architectures."

**Impact:**
- Increased deployment time and cost
- Ongoing maintenance challenges
- System underutilization

## Use Cases for Correlation Discovery

### Use Case 1: Occupancy-HVAC Optimization

**Current State:**
- HVAC runs on fixed schedule (6am-8pm)
- Actual occupancy varies significantly
- Energy wasted on empty spaces

**With Edge Intelligence:**
- Discovers occupancy patterns from motion, CO2, WiFi
- Learns HVAC response time per zone
- Predicts occupancy 30-60 minutes ahead
- Pre-conditions only occupied zones
- **Savings: 20-30%**

### Use Case 2: Weather-Building Envelope Correlation

**Current State:**
- Building responds reactively to temperature
- No consideration of thermal mass, orientation
- Overcompensation wastes energy

**With Edge Intelligence:**
- Discovers solar gain patterns by zone/time
- Learns thermal mass response curves
- Predicts interior temp from outdoor forecast
- Preemptive conditioning optimization
- **Savings: 10-15%**

### Use Case 3: Multi-System Coordination

**Current State:**
- HVAC, lighting, blinds controlled independently
- Actions conflict (cooling on while sun heating)
- No holistic optimization

**With Edge Intelligence:**
- Discovers cross-system correlations
- Learns: "Closing blinds reduces cooling load by X"
- Coordinates actions for optimal comfort + efficiency
- **Savings: 15-25%**

### Use Case 4: Predictive Maintenance

**Current State:**
- Equipment fails unexpectedly
- Reactive maintenance costs 3-5x preventive
- Downtime affects tenant satisfaction

**With Edge Intelligence:**
- Discovers performance degradation patterns
- Learns: "Motor vibration + high current = failure in 2 weeks"
- Alerts before failure
- **Savings: 25-40% on maintenance**

### Use Case 5: Demand Response Optimization

**Current State:**
- Peak demand charges = 30-50% of electric bill
- Manual load shedding is crude
- Comfort is sacrificed

**With Edge Intelligence:**
- Predicts peak demand 30-60 minutes ahead
- Learns which loads can shift without comfort impact
- Automated pre-cooling/pre-heating
- **Savings: 10-20% on demand charges**
