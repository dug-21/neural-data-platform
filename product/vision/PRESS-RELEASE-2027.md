### Neural Data Platform Ships: $75 Device Brings Self-Learning Intelligence to Any Industry

**The first edge computer that discovers patterns, learns causation, and takes action—without cloud, without subscriptions, without experts**

---

**SEATTLE** — Today marks the general availability of the Neural Data Platform (NDP), a $75 device that brings autonomous intelligence to farms, factories, buildings, and clinics worldwide. Unlike cloud-dependent systems costing thousands per year, NDP learns entirely on-device, discovers correlations humans miss, and improves forever—with zero monthly fees.

"We asked a simple question," said the project's creator. "Why does a greenhouse controller cost $25,000 when the intelligence could run on a $75 computer? Why do small manufacturers pay $500 per machine per month for monitoring that still requires experts to interpret? The answer is: it doesn't have to."

**The technology gap NDP closes is significant.** Today, 90% of commercial buildings lack smart automation. Only 27% of farms use precision agriculture. Small manufacturers lose $10,000-50,000 per hour to unplanned downtime but can't afford predictive maintenance. Researchers face 50% funding cuts while monitoring equipment costs $50,000 per site. In developing regions, 730 million people lack electricity and 62% lack internet—making cloud-dependent solutions useless.

NDP addresses all of these with a device that:

- **Discovers correlations automatically.** Connect any sensors. NDP finds relationships you didn't know existed—like how humidity plus door timing predicts equipment failure, or which weather patterns precede yield drops.

- **Validates what's actually causal.** Not all correlations matter. NDP learns which patterns drive real outcomes, eliminating false signals.

- **Predicts and acts.** Once NDP understands your environment, it forecasts future states and triggers actions to achieve your declared objectives—maintaining air quality, preventing downtime, optimizing energy.

- **Runs entirely offline.** No cloud. No subscriptions. No data leaving your premises. Critical for rural farms, remote research sites, developing regions, and anyone who values data sovereignty.

- **Gets smarter forever.** NDP learns your specific environment. A greenhouse in Arizona develops different intelligence than one in Maine. Your factory floor patterns are unique to your machines, your operators, your jobs.

**Early deployments span seven industries:**

A 50-employee machine shop in Ohio reduced unplanned downtime by 34% after NDP discovered that coolant temperature plus spindle vibration predicted bearing failure three days in advance—a pattern their $2,000/month monitoring system had missed.

A vertical farm in Singapore cut energy costs 23% when NDP found that adjusting LED timing based on external light levels (correlated through cloud cover patterns) reduced HVAC load without affecting yield.

A senior living facility in Florida now receives 6-hour advance warning of resident fall risk after NDP correlated bathroom timing, sleep disruption, and barometric pressure with incident reports—without any cloud transmission of health data.

A research station in Kenya deployed 47 environmental monitors on a grant that previously funded three, enabling watershed-scale data collection for the first time.

"The system found things we never would have looked for," said the facilities director at the Florida senior living community. "We knew falls happened more at night. We didn't know they happened more on low-pressure weather days, or that certain bathroom visit patterns preceded them by hours. Now we do."

**NDP is available today** as open-source software for Raspberry Pi 5, with turnkey hardware bundles for specific industries starting at $150. There are no subscriptions, no cloud requirements, and no licensing fees.

The platform supports hundreds of sensors across any data domain, with pre-built adapters for environmental monitoring, industrial equipment, building systems, and agricultural sensors. Custom domains can be added through a declarative configuration system that requires no programming.

Documentation and community support are available at [project website]. Enterprise support packages are available for multi-site deployments.

### FAQ Preparation

**Q: How is this different from Home Assistant?**
A: Home Assistant is rules-based—you tell it what to do. NDP discovers what to do by learning your environment.

**Q: What about security?**
A: Your data never leaves the device. There's no cloud to breach.

**Q: Can it really run complex ML on a Raspberry Pi?**
A: Yes. Quantized models (INT8) and efficient architectures (TCN) enable real inference in milliseconds.

**Q: What if I need support?**
A: Community support is free. Enterprise support packages are available for critical deployments.
