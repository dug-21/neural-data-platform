**Implemented Models:**

- **DeepAR:** Probabilistic forecasting (100→50→25 neurons)
- **Transformer:** Attention-based architecture (256→128→64→32 neurons)
- **MLP:** Multi-Layer Perceptron baseline (64→32→16 neurons)
